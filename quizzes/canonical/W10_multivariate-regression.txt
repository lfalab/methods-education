QUESTION: Which of the following equations represents a multivariate regression?

A. Y = b0 + b1*X1 + b2*X2
B. Y = b0 + b1*X
C. Y = b0 * b1 * X1 * X2
D. Y = b0 + b1*X1 * b2*X2

ANSWER: A

QUESTION: In a multivariate regression, a researcher reports that the slope on education is -1.14, "holding income constant." What does "holding income constant" mean?

A. The slope compares individuals (or units) with similar levels of income to isolate the relationship between education and the outcome
B. Income was removed from the dataset before running the regression
C. Income does not vary in the sample
D. The researcher fixed income at its mean value and re-ran the analysis

ANSWER: A

QUESTION: Researchers find that ice cream sales predict crime rates. When they add temperature to the model, the coefficient on ice cream sales drops to nearly zero. What role does temperature play in this example?

B. Temperature is caused by ice cream sales
C. Temperature is the dependent variable
D. Temperature is a mediating variable on the pathway from ice cream sales to crime

QUESTION: How does adding a control variable to a regression help address confounding?

A. It compares units that are similar on the control variable, so the coefficient on X reflects its relationship with Y among comparable units
B. It removes the control variable from the dataset entirely
C. It guarantees that the remaining coefficients are causal
D. It increases the sample size

QUESTION: A study finds that owning a gym membership predicts better health outcomes. When income is added to the model, the coefficient on gym membership shrinks dramatically. What does this suggest?

B. Gym memberships cause higher income
C. Income is unrelated to both gym memberships and health
D. The original finding was due to measurement error

QUESTION: A researcher adds several control variables to a regression and finds that the coefficient on the main explanatory variable remains statistically significant. Can the researcher conclude that the relationship is causal?

B. Yes, because controlling for confounders eliminates all alternative explanations
