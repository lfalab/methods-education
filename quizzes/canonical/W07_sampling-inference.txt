QUESTION: A researcher wants to know the average income of all households in London. They survey 500 randomly selected households. What is the population in this study?

A. The 500 households surveyed
B. All households in London
C. All households in the United Kingdom
D. All residents of London

ANSWER: B

QUESTION: A television network conducts a poll by asking viewers to text their opinions on a political issue. Which statement best describes this sampling method?

A. This is a probability sample because every viewer has a chance to respond
B. This is a non-probability sample and cannot support a valid inference about the general population
C. This is a random sample because viewers choose whether to participate
D. This is representative because the network has many viewers

ANSWER: B

---

QUESTION: In a simple random sample, which statement is correct?

A. Every unit in the population has an equal probability of being selected
B. Only units with certain characteristics can be selected
C. The sample is guaranteed to be identical to the population
D. Larger units have a higher probability of being selected

ANSWER: A

QUESTION: A poll estimates that 58% of voters support a policy, with a margin of error of ±4%. What is the 95% confidence interval for this estimate?

A. [54%, 62%]
B. [58%, 62%]
C. [50%, 66%]
D. [54%, 58%]

ANSWER: A

QUESTION: Two polls estimate support for the same candidate. Poll A finds 47% ± 3%, and Poll B finds 51% ± 3%. Which statement is correct?

QUESTION: A survey of 800 randomly selected adults finds that 35% support a proposed law, with a 95% confidence interval of [32%, 38%]. What does this confidence interval mean?

QUESTION: A study compares average exam scores between two teaching methods. The 95% confidence interval for the difference is [−2.1, +8.4] points. The null hypothesis states that there is no difference between methods. What should the researcher conclude?

B. Fail to reject the null hypothesis because the interval includes zero
C. Reject the null hypothesis because the interval includes negative values
D. Accept the null hypothesis because zero is in the interval

QUESTION: Researchers test whether men and women differ in their average hours worked per week. The 95% confidence interval for the difference (women − men) is [+2.3, +6.8] hours. What conclusion is justified?

A. Reject the null hypothesis of no difference
B. Fail to reject the null hypothesis of no difference

QUESTION: A confidence interval for the difference in turnout rates between two regions is [−1.2%, +3.8%]. The null hypothesis states that there is no difference. If the researchers fail to reject the null, what can they conclude?

QUESTION: What does a p-value measure?

A. The probability that the null hypothesis is true
B. The probability of observing results at least as extreme as ours, assuming the null hypothesis is true
C. The probability that our sample is representative
D. The percentage of the population that supports the alternative hypothesis

ANSWER: B

QUESTION: A study reports a p-value of 0.03. Using a significance level of α = 0.05, what should the researcher conclude?

A. Fail to reject the null hypothesis
B. Reject the null hypothesis
C. Accept the null hypothesis
D. The result is inconclusive

ANSWER: B

QUESTION: A researcher obtains a p-value of 0.12 when testing whether two groups differ in their average spending. What does this p-value indicate?

B. The observed difference would not be particularly unusual if the null hypothesis were true
C. There is a 12% probability that the null hypothesis is true

QUESTION: Which statement correctly describes the relationship between p-values and 95% confidence intervals?
