QUESTION: A regression equation is given as: y = 10 + 3x. What is the predicted value of y when x = 5?

A. 13
B. 15
C. 25
D. 50

ANSWER: C

---

QUESTION: In the regression equation y = 20 − 2x, what does the slope tell us?

A. For each 1-unit increase in x, y is expected to decrease by 2 units
B. For each 1-unit increase in y, x is expected to decrease by 2 units
C. When x = 0, y equals 2
D. For each 1-unit increase in x, y is expected to increase by 2 units

ANSWER: A

---

QUESTION: A regression line slopes downward from left to right on a scatterplot. What does this indicate about the relationship between x and y?

A. As x increases, y tends to increase
B. As x increases, y tends to decrease
C. x and y are unrelated
D. The regression equation has a positive intercept

ANSWER: B

QUESTION: In a regression of vote share on campaign spending, the equation is: Vote Share = 35 + 0.8 × Spending (where spending is in thousands of pounds). What is the intercept?

A. 0.8
B. 35
C. 35.8
D. 28

ANSWER: B

QUESTION: What does the intercept in a regression equation represent?

A. The predicted value of y when x = 0
B. The change in y per unit increase in x
C. The predicted value of x when y = 0
D. The average value of y

ANSWER: A

QUESTION: A researcher estimates the regression: Income = 15,000 + 2,500 × Years of Education. What does the coefficient 2,500 mean?

A. When education is zero, income is £2,500
B. For each additional year of education, income is expected to increase by £2,500
C. The average income in the sample is £2,500
D. Education explains 2,500 units of income variation

ANSWER: B

QUESTION: In a regression of exam scores on hours studied, the intercept is 40. If no students in the sample studied fewer than 5 hours, what can we say about the intercept?

A. It represents a meaningful prediction for students who did not study
B. It should be interpreted with caution as it lies outside the range of the data
C. It is incorrect and should be zero
D. It indicates the minimum possible exam score

ANSWER: B

QUESTION: What is a residual in regression analysis?

A. The difference between the predicted value and the actual value for an observation
B. The slope of the regression line
C. The proportion of variance explained by the model
D. The sum of all predicted values

ANSWER: A

---

QUESTION: Ordinary Least Squares (OLS) estimation finds the regression line by:

A. Maximising the sum of squared residuals
B. Minimising the sum of squared residuals
C. Setting all residuals equal to zero
D. Maximising the correlation between x and y

ANSWER: B

QUESTION: In regression hypothesis testing, what does the null hypothesis typically state?

A. The slope equals zero
B. The intercept equals zero
C. The slope equals one
D. R-squared equals zero

ANSWER: A

---

QUESTION: A regression of turnout on age yields a slope of 0.4 with a 95% confidence interval of [0.1, 0.7]. What conclusion is justified?

A. Reject the null hypothesis that the slope equals zero
B. Fail to reject the null hypothesis that the slope equals zero
C. Accept that age has no effect on turnout
D. The relationship is not statistically significant

ANSWER: A

---

QUESTION: A regression slope has a 95% confidence interval of [−0.3, +0.5]. What does this tell us about the null hypothesis that the slope equals zero?

A. We reject the null hypothesis
B. We fail to reject the null hypothesis
C. The slope is definitely positive
D. The slope is definitely zero

ANSWER: B

---

QUESTION: A regression of support for a policy on income produces a slope of 0.02 with a p-value of 0.001. Using α = 0.05, what should we conclude?

A. Fail to reject the null hypothesis
B. Reject the null hypothesis
C. Accept the null hypothesis
D. The result is not statistically significant

ANSWER: B

QUESTION: Which of the following would make a regression slope more likely to be statistically significant?

A. A smaller sample size
B. More scatter around the regression line
C. A steeper slope (further from zero)
D. Less spread in the independent variable

ANSWER: C

QUESTION: What does an R-squared value of 0.45 mean?

A. 45% of the variance in y is explained by x
B. The slope equals 0.45
C. 45% of observations lie on the regression line
D. The correlation between x and y is 0.45

QUESTION: A regression has an R-squared of 0.15. Which statement is correct?

A. The regression is invalid because R-squared is too low
B. The slope must be statistically insignificant

D. The model is incorrectly specified

ANSWER: C

QUESTION: Which statement about R-squared is correct?

A. R-squared can take any value from negative infinity to positive infinity
B. R-squared always falls between 0 and 1
C. R-squared measures the strength of the causal relationship
D. A higher R-squared always means the slope is more statistically significant

QUESTION: A regression of health spending (dependent variable) on GDP (independent variable) has R-squared = 0.72 and a statistically significant slope. What can we conclude?

A. GDP causes health spending to increase
B. 72% of the variance in health spending is explained by GDP
C. The relationship between GDP and health spending is definitely causal
D. Health spending does not explain any variance in GDP

ANSWER: B

QUESTION: Which statement correctly distinguishes between statistical significance and R-squared?

A. Statistical significance tests whether the slope differs from zero; R-squared measures how much variance is explained
B. R-squared tests whether the slope differs from zero; statistical significance measures variance explained
C. They measure the same thing using different scales
D. Statistical significance is only relevant when R-squared is above 0.5

ANSWER: A

QUESTION: A researcher finds a statistically significant slope with p = 0.01 but R-squared = 0.08. Which interpretation is most accurate?

A. The low R-squared proves the slope estimate is wrong
B. There is strong evidence of a relationship, but other factors also matter
C. The result contradicts itself and cannot be trusted
D. Statistical significance is impossible with such a low R-squared
