Why are we focusing on empirical questions?

- Although normative questions are important, they cannot be answered with data alone
- Normative questions don't matter that much
- Empirical questions are always more interesting than normative questions

Why should an empirical social scientist avoid researching whether Bernie Sanders would have beaten Donald Trump in 2016?

- There's no way to know if Bernie would have won
- If Bernie had won, the world would still be the same today
- Bernie obviously would have won
