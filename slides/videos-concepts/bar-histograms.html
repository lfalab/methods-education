Recap

  • Bar charts and histograms both visualise distributions.
  • Bar charts are used for nominal variables and show counts by category.
  • Histograms are used for ordinal, interval, and ratio variables and show counts within ranges of values.

Why this matters:

  • Using the wrong type of graph can obscure patterns or suggest structure that is not there.
  • Choosing the right visualization helps us see what is typical, what is rare, and how values are distributed.
